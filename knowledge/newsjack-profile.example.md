# Standing profile (example: Brightwater Analytics)

The newsjack skills (`skills-shared/newsjack/`) decide whether a brand has honest STANDING on a story before writing angles. This file is that brand's profile. "Standing" means the brand, or its named spokesperson, can say something specific that a reader would not get from the news itself.

Everything below is FABRICATED. Brightwater Analytics is not a real company. Copy this file, rename it, and replace every section with your own brand's facts.

## Who
- Brightwater Analytics: a small consultancy that helps mid-size water utilities plan sensor networks and leak detection. Based in a fictional coastal city, Port Halden.
- Spokesperson: Dana Reyes, founder. Brand handle: @brightwater_example.
- Track record (usable as proof, stated plainly): sensor rollout plans for 14 municipal utilities; an open dataset of 3 years of anonymized pipe-burst events; a yearly "state of urban leak loss" report.

## Strong standing (the brand can add a specific, first-hand read)
- Water utility infrastructure spending and grant programs.
- Leak detection and smart-meter technology, especially for mid-size utilities.
- Drought and water-loss policy where it changes how utilities budget for sensors.
- Proof asset: the open burst-event dataset and the yearly report.

## Partial standing (only with a clear utility-operations angle)
- General climate and drought news: only via "what it means for a mid-size utility's budget".
- Smart-city and IoT news: only where water networks are involved.
- Big infrastructure bills: only the water-system line items.

## No standing (route to watch, do not write angles)
- Stock moves of large engineering firms, consumer gadget launches, celebrity news, national politics without a water-infrastructure link.

## Brand safety (any hit = kill or route to watch)
- The brand sells planning advice, not engineering certification or legal advice. Never phrase an angle as a certification or a legal opinion.
- No accusations against a named utility or official.
- No forward money or savings estimates ("this will save $X").
- No name-drops of people or firms in claims about misconduct.
- Do not speculate on enforcement against a named company.

## Formats the team can ship
- Single social post (default, at most 1 a day), thread (rare), carousel for explainers (concrete numbers, local framing), newsletter item. A person approves every draft before posting.
