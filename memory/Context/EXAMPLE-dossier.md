> **Example file.** This shows the shape of a Tier-2 subject dossier (`memory/Context/`). A dossier
> is the on-demand recall record for one SUBJECT: a deal, a partner, a project, a person, a recurring
> theme. Copy it to `memory/Context/<subject-slug>.md`, replace the fabricated content below, and add
> a one-line pointer in `memory/MEMORY.md`. The two-part shape (compiled truth + timeline) is the
> point: the top is the current synthesized answer, the bottom is the append-only evidence trail.
> Delete this quote-block in your real file.

# <Subject Name>

## Compiled truth (current state)

> The synthesized, current answer to "what do we know about this subject?" Rewrite this section as
> understanding changes, it always reflects NOW. If someone reads only this section, they should have
> the accurate current picture without scrolling the timeline.

- **Status**: `<one word: active / signed / stalled / walked / closed>`
- **Summary**: `<2-4 sentences of the current state of play>`
- **Key facts**: `<the load-bearing numbers, names (genericized for any public surface), terms>`
- **Open question / next action**: `<what is unresolved and who owns it>`

### Worked example (fabricated, replace)
- **Status**: active
- **Summary**: Northwind Foundation is evaluating a market-entry engagement. Scoped to a bounded
  pilot first, broader retainer deferred until the pilot proves out. Primary contact prefers async.
- **Key facts**: pilot budget ~`$X`, single decision-maker, timeline gated on their Q2 planning.
- **Open question**: whether the pilot deliverable is fixed-scope or milestone-based. awaiting their
  reply. no action needed until they respond.

## Timeline (append-only)

> Dated events, oldest first (or newest first, pick one and stay consistent). This is the evidence
> trail the compiled-truth section is synthesized from. Never edit past entries, append new ones.

- **2026-01-10** — first contact via a warm intro. inbound interest, no commercials discussed.
- **2026-01-13** — discovery call. surfaced the real constraint (their planning cycle). scoped a
  bounded pilot as the low-risk entry.
- **2026-01-15** — sent the pilot outline. flagged the fixed-vs-milestone question as the one open
  decision. moved status to active, awaiting-reply.
